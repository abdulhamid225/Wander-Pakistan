A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Travel well.
Leave gently.

A little preparation can make a big difference. Keep these basics in mind before heading out.

Start planning →
01 Pick the right season +

Northern routes can be seasonal, with snow and closures in colder months. Coastal and southern destinations have different heat and rainfall patterns. Check local guidance before departure.

02 Pack in layers +

Mountain weather can change quickly. Bring layers, comfortable footwear, sun protection, water, and any personal medication you need.

03 Respect local customs +

Dress thoughtfully, ask before photographing people, follow site rules, and be considerate around religious and community spaces.

04 Take altitude seriously +

Give yourself time to acclimatize at high elevations. If you feel unwell at altitude, stop ascending and seek local medical advice.

05 Plan for the road +

Road conditions, access, and travel times can change. Verify conditions with local authorities or trusted operators shortly before you go, and keep flexible plans.

06 Leave no trace +

Carry out your rubbish, avoid disturbing wildlife, stay on established paths, and support local businesses and guides where possible.
